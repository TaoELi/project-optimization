### Open hypotheses
- Target convergence control rather than raw contraction vectorization: investigate AH/CIAH preconditioning, keyframe restart conditions, and step acceptance so the benchmark needs fewer Hessian-vector calls or restarts without changing the final scientific result.
- If revisiting contraction speed, first collect full-run timing around `h_op`/AH phases and memory allocation behavior to prove the candidate affects the measured `weighted_median_casscf_kernel_seconds`; the first two contraction-focused candidates were slower, and iteration 5's split-density JK algebra was effectively neutral.
- A terminal no-step skip is not enough by itself; look for an earlier convergence-control change that removes one keyframe restart/H-op batch or one macro-level AO2MO while still recomputing authoritative energies when a real orbital/CI step has occurred.
- Do not simply relax the terminal AH gradient stop in `update_orb_ci`; iteration 4 showed this can buy the desired speedup but changes the final active-space energy. A safer direction is adaptive AH effort that remains strict for final/keyframe acceptance while trying to reduce early wasted H-op calls through preconditioning or restart criteria.
- After iteration 6, prioritize instrumentation-driven work inside AH/direct-JK scheduling over further `dm1`/support reassociation. The target likely needs a structural reduction in H-op calls, keyframe restarts, or expensive JK builds, not another sub-percent dense-array optimization.
- After iteration 7, the biggest proven lever is the JK backend used inside AH response contractions, not the number of AH iterations. With DF-AH accepted, the remaining large costs are DF AH response time and exact AO2MO time, both near 24s in the authoritative summary; future candidates should either reduce DF setup/repeated response overhead or trim exact AO2MO work without weakening the final energy comparison.
- Validate any follow-up against the strict no-scientific-target-change rule: DF is acceptable here because it is confined to the AH response path and the exact final energies match tolerance. Do not extend approximations into authoritative AO2MO/CASCI energy construction unless the benchmark explicitly allows it.
- After iteration 8, AH response time is no longer tied with AO2MO; factorized DF-JK lowered `weighted_median_ah_seconds` to 17.856789063s while exact AO2MO remains 23.624273920s. The next high-value target is exact AO2MO cost or memory movement around DF loops, not changing convergence tolerances.
- Preserve the large-AO guard or improve it empirically. The low-rank path is valuable for ccpVTZ-scale AO spaces, but worker checks showed dense DF-JK can still win for small AO/rank cases; future generalization should use a measured or complexity-based threshold rather than forcing factorization everywhere.
- After iteration 9, avoid simply concatenating all low-rank response factors into wider per-block transforms. - After iteration 10, unpacked-block/A-transform caching alone is below threshold and increases memory footprint. If revisiting it, make the cache smaller or streamed enough to reduce RSS and pair it with a separate end-to-end win; otherwise focus on exact AO2MO cost, which is now larger than AH in the accepted incumbent and this candidate.
